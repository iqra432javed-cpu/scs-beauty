FAQs

Got Questions?

Is there a joining fee to become a partner?
No. Joining the SCS Partner Program is completely free. You only invest in products when you're ready to start selling.
Do I need prior sales or beauty experience?
Not at all. Our comprehensive training program teaches you everything — from skin science to selling skills. We start from scratch with you.
How and when do I receive my earnings?
Earnings are paid on a commission basis per sale. As a partner, you collect payment from customers directly and receive your margin immediately.
Can I work part-time alongside my current job?
Absolutely. Many of our top partners started part-time. You set your own hours and build at your own pace — there's no minimum target to join.
What products will I be selling?
SCS offers a complete range of organic skincare — serums, creams, polish, sunblock, and treatments. All products sell themselves because they genuinely work.
Is SCS available in my city?
We deliver and support partners in 150+ cities across Pakistan — from Karachi to Gilgit. If you're in Pakistan, you can join and sell with us.
