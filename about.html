The Beginning

How SCS Began

Success Chaser Stars was founded in 2020 with a simple but powerful mission: to make premium, organic skincare accessible to every woman in Pakistan — especially those who had been let down by harsh chemical products that damaged their skin.

Our founder noticed a gap in the Pakistani market. International luxury brands were either too expensive or not formulated for South Asian skin tones and concerns — hyperpigmentation, sun damage, melasma, and acne being among the most common.

"Why should Pakistani women settle for products not made for them? We decided to change that — one serum at a time."

What started as a passion project with a handful of products quickly grew into Pakistan's most trusted organic beauty community — with over 5,000 certified partners and 50,000 happy customers across 150+ cities.
