Delivery Timeframes

  • Major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad): 2–4 business days
  • Other cities & towns: 4–7 business days
  • Remote areas (FATA, Gilgit-Baltistan, AJK): 6–10 business days

Business days are Monday–Saturday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed after 6 PM are processed the next business day.

Courier Partners

We ship via Pakistan's most reliable courier services:

  • TCS Couriers
  • Leopards Courier
  • M&P Express

Tracking information will be shared via WhatsApp once your order is dispatched.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

  • Payment is made directly to the courier at time of delivery.
  • Please have the exact amount ready. Couriers may not carry change.
  • Do not pay any advance payment unless officially confirmed by SCS via our verified WhatsApp number.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges depend on your city and order size. Current charges will be confirmed at the time of order via WhatsApp. We regularly run free delivery promotions — ask your consultant!

Failed Delivery

  • If you are not available at time of delivery, the courier will attempt redelivery.
  • After 2 failed attempts, the order may be returned to us. A redelivery fee may apply.
  • Ensure your WhatsApp number is active so the courier can reach you.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong product, contact us immediately via WhatsApp with photos. We will arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.
